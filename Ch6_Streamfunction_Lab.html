Streamfunction Lab

For incompressible plane flow one scalar field ψ(x, y) carries the whole velocity field: u = ∂ψ/∂y, v = −∂ψ/∂x. Its contours are the streamlines, and the difference in ψ between two streamlines is the flowrate passing between them.
